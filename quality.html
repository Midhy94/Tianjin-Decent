For critical load-bearing scaffolding and structural steel, metallurgical compliance is non-negotiable. Every production batch is certified to international standards prior to container dispatch at Port of Tianjin.

MTC EN 10204 3.1 / 3.2

Traceable heat records & mechanical validation.

A Mill Test Certificate (MTC) provides the complete metallurgical lineage of your steel. Issued in accordance with EN 10204 Type 3.1 (or Type 3.2 with independent third-party witness), our certificates confirm:

  • • Chemical Composition: Carbon (C), Manganese (Mn), Silicon (Si), Phosphorus (P), Sulfur (S), and Carbon Equivalent Value (CEV).
  • • Mechanical Properties: Yield strength (ReH / Rp0.2), Tensile strength (Rm), Percentage elongation (A80 / A5), and Charpy V-notch impact energy at -20°C / 0°C.
  • • Dimensional Verification: Calipered thickness tolerances, camber, squareness, and flatness measurements.

INDEPENDENT VERIFICATION

Third-party laboratory inspection.

We coordinate pre-shipment inspections with world-renowned certification agencies at our Tianjin logistics warehouses.

AUDIT 01

SGS Pre-Shipment Inspection

Randomized piece-count verification, wall-thickness micrometer testing, galvanization coating thickness magnetic testing (ISO 2178), and visual weld examination.

AUDIT 02

Bureau Veritas (BV) Witness

Tensile pull testing on scaffold couplers (EN 74-1 slip resistance testing to 15 kN), rosette load-bearing shear tests on Ring Lock standards, and drop-weight impact verification.

AUDIT 03

Container Sealing & Stuffing

Supervised container packing, weight bridge verification, desiccant pouch placement, moisture barrier wrapping, and formal customs seal application.
